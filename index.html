Vomcore · Contraction Journal

Contraction timer and session log

A private, on-device record of contraction start and end times, duration, intervals and session summaries. This is a recording tool. It does not diagnose labour or decide when to seek care.

Help with your records

Tap Start when a contraction begins, then Stop when it ends. Duration is the time between those two actions. “Start to next start” includes the contraction itself. “End to next start” measures the rest between contractions.

Tap a record to correct its times or delete it. Undo restores the last change. End the current session before starting a separate one. History keeps your previous sessions on this device. Use Share in a session summary to choose where to send a copy.

The timer recovers a running record after the app closes. If a device restart or time change prevents a reliable calculation, the app asks you to review the times instead of inventing a duration. A missing end time requires your correction.

Purchases

Basic timing, record editing and basic sharing are free. Pro is an optional one-time purchase for starred sessions, customized summary fields and CSV export. Its price comes from the App Store. To restore a previous purchase, finish any open session, open Settings, then Pro and Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used to buy it.
