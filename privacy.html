Contraction Journal privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026. This policy applies to Vomcore's Contraction Timer: Session Log app and its localized names, bundle identifier com.vomcore.contractionjournal.

Your records stay on your device

Contraction start and end times, durations, intervals, session history, corrections and starred records are saved in the app's local storage. The app excludes its journal file from device backups. It does not upload these records to Vomcore, an analytics provider or a health service. No account, name, pregnancy details, location, contacts or HealthKit permission is required. There is no advertising, tracking SDK or in-app analytics service.

The app uses the device's continuous clock and a boot-session marker to recover a running timer and detect when a duration cannot be calculated reliably. The boot marker stays in local storage and is not included in exported summaries.

Sharing is your choice

A record leaves the app only when you choose Share or Export and select a destination. A summary or CSV file contains the session information shown by the app. The receiving app or person controls their copy. Temporary CSV files created for sharing are removed when the share sheet closes; iOS can also remove temporary files.

Purchases and Apple services

Optional Pro purchases and restoration use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment and purchase information under Apple's privacy policy. The app reads verified purchase entitlements to unlock Pro; it does not receive your payment-card details. Purchase and restore actions do not change or send your contraction records. We do not operate a purchase server or use RevenueCat.

Deleting data

You can delete individual records in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its local journal. There is no Vomcore cloud copy to recover. Copies that you have shared must be deleted separately at their destinations.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the address and content you send so that we can respond. Avoid sending health records unless you deliberately choose to include them. This documentation site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary web request information such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement. The site has no added analytics, forms, advertising or cookies.

Contact and changes

Contact hello@vomcore.com for privacy questions. We will update the date and policy if the app's data behaviour changes.
